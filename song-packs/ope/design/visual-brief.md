# オペ Visual Brief

## Main Structure

An operating room is invaded by public records, news-like judgment, and outside evaluation pressure. The main shape is a tilted procedure table under offset clinical lamps, surrounded by monitors, triage boards, diagnosis panels, verdict cards, and suture-like UI paths. The song's pressure comes from public judgment and the urge to pull life back from that judgment, so the visual should feel clinical, urgent, and over-documented rather than theatrical.

## Avoid

- Do not use direct gore, explicit self-harm imagery, or medical realism.
- Do not copy the official MV frames, characters, photos, screenshots, or typography.
- Do not use live-stage, concert, runway, curtain, spotlight, or audience imagery.
- Do not rely on a centered radial glow, light network, or particle field as the main composition.
- Do not make the chorus only "brighter"; change the reading of the screen.
